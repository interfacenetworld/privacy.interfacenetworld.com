# Interface Networld™ Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** September 13, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Interface Networld Software Corporation ("Interface Networld," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies across InterfaceNetworld.com and related Services that link to this policy.

> Deployment note: Review this policy against the final production analytics, advertising, consent-management, and third-party integrations before launch.

## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on or associated with your browser or device. They can support authentication, security, preferences, analytics, performance, and advertising. Similar technologies may include local storage, software development kits, pixels, tags, or device identifiers.

## 2. Cookies We May Use

### Strictly Necessary Cookies
These are required for core functions such as account authentication, security, session management, load balancing, and fraud prevention. They cannot always be disabled without affecting the Service.

The current Interface account system uses:

- **`interface_session`** — a secure, HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax session cookie used to keep a user signed in. The current implementation may persist for up to 30 days unless the user logs out earlier or the session is otherwise revoked or expired.

### Preference Cookies
These may remember choices such as language, appearance, interface settings, accessibility preferences, or other personalized options.

### Analytics and Performance Cookies
If enabled, these may help us understand usage patterns, diagnose problems, measure performance, and improve products. Analytics cookies should be activated only in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

### Advertising or Targeting Cookies
If advertising features are enabled, these technologies may be used to measure campaigns, limit repeated ads, personalize advertising, or understand interactions with advertisements. Where required by law, non-essential advertising technologies will be subject to consent or opt-out controls.

## 3. First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

A first-party cookie is set by an Interface Networld domain. A third-party cookie or similar technology may be provided by an external analytics, advertising, payment, media, social, security, or infrastructure provider.

Third-party providers control their own technologies and may process information under their own privacy terms.

## 4. Cookie Consent and Preferences

Where required, Interface Networld will provide a cookie banner or preference center that allows users to:

- accept all optional cookies;
- reject non-essential cookies;
- choose cookie categories; and
- change preferences later.

Strictly necessary technologies may operate without optional consent where permitted because they are needed to provide a requested service or maintain security.

## 5. Browser Controls

Most browsers let you block, delete, or restrict cookies through browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies can prevent sign-in, account persistence, or other site functions from operating correctly.

## 6. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Signals

Where legally required and technically supported, Interface Networld may recognize applicable browser-based opt-out preference signals, such as the Global Privacy Control, for processing covered by those laws. Browser "Do Not Track" signals do not have a single universally adopted technical or legal standard.

## 7. Retention

Cookie duration varies by purpose. Session cookies may expire when a session ends; persistent cookies may remain until their configured expiration or until removed by the user. The current `interface_session` cookie is configured with a maximum age of up to 30 days.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when technologies, vendors, product features, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date will identify the latest revision.

## 9. Contact

For cookie or privacy questions, contact Interface Networld through the Contact page on **InterfaceNetworld.com**.

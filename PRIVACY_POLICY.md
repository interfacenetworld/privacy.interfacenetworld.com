# Interface Networld™ Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 13, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

Interface Networld Software Corporation ("Interface Networld," "we," "us," or "our") respects privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, stored, and protected when you use InterfaceNetworld.com, related Interface Networld websites, applications, developer services, APIs, cloud services, and other products that link to this Policy (collectively, the "Services").

> Deployment note: This policy should be reviewed against the final production data flows, vendors, advertising configuration, payment providers, and jurisdiction-specific requirements before public launch.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the Services you use, we may collect:

- **Account information:** email address, display name, account identifiers, account status, and authentication records.
- **Authentication and security information:** password hashes and salts, session identifiers, API-key metadata, login events, security events, and audit logs. We do not need to store your raw account password to authenticate you.
- **Device and technical information:** browser or app information, user-agent data, IP-derived security or network information, operating system, device type, request metadata, timestamps, and diagnostic data.
- **Content and files you provide:** images, videos, audio, documents, prompts, project files, generated media, and other material you upload, create, store, or submit through the Services.
- **Usage information:** features used, interactions, requests, application activity, and service-performance information.
- **Developer information:** API-key names, key prefixes, scopes, creation dates, last-used dates, and related developer-account records.
- **Communications:** information you provide when you contact support, submit feedback, join a waitlist, or communicate with us.
- **Transaction information:** if paid services are introduced or used, billing status, subscription information, and payment-related records. Payment-card information may be processed directly by a payment provider rather than stored by Interface Networld.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Services;
- create and manage accounts, sessions, API keys, and private storage;
- process user requests, uploads, media, and AI-generation workflows;
- secure accounts, detect abuse, investigate incidents, and prevent fraud;
- personalize product experiences and remember user preferences;
- monitor reliability, performance, and usage trends;
- communicate service updates, security notices, support responses, and product information;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable agreements; and
- develop new Interface Networld products, features, models, and platform capabilities where permitted by law and consistent with applicable user controls and contractual commitments.

## 3. AI, Generated Content, and Uploaded Media

Some Interface Networld products may use artificial intelligence, computer vision, audio processing, generative models, or other automated systems. Content you submit may be processed to provide the feature you requested, such as generating, editing, analyzing, storing, rendering, or transforming content.

Where third-party infrastructure or model providers are used, data may be transmitted to those providers solely as needed to perform the requested service, subject to their contractual and security obligations. Product-specific notices may provide additional details.

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for functions such as authentication, security, preferences, analytics, and—if enabled—advertising. The current Interface account system uses a secure HTTP-only session cookie named `interface_session` to maintain a signed-in session. See the Interface Networld Cookie Policy for more information.

## 5. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to:

- **service providers and infrastructure partners** that host, secure, process, store, analyze, or support the Services;
- **payment providers** when processing purchases or subscriptions;
- **professional advisers** such as lawyers, accountants, auditors, or insurers when reasonably necessary;
- **law-enforcement, regulators, courts, or other parties** when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the Services;
- **business transaction participants** in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, restructuring, bankruptcy, or sale of assets; and
- **other parties at your direction or with your consent.**

We do not sell personal information for money. Depending on the analytics or advertising technologies enabled on a particular Service, certain disclosures may be treated as a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under some privacy laws. Where required, we will provide applicable choice or opt-out mechanisms.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security and audit records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary depending on data type and purpose.

User files may remain stored until deleted by the user, removed under an applicable retention rule, or deleted as part of account termination, subject to backup, legal, fraud-prevention, or security requirements.

## 7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. Current platform security measures include encrypted HTTPS transport, secure HTTP-only session cookies, hashed authentication secrets, access controls, private object storage, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale, or sharing; or to appeal certain privacy decisions.

To exercise a request, contact Interface Networld through the Contact page on InterfaceNetworld.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Authorized-agent requests may require additional verification where permitted by law.

## 9. Account and File Controls

Where supported, signed-in users may manage account sessions, API keys, private files, and other stored content through Interface Networld account and developer tools. Deleting content from an active interface may not immediately remove copies retained temporarily for backup, security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

The general Interface Networld Services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through general-audience Services without appropriate authorization. Specific products may impose a higher minimum age.

## 11. International Data Transfers

Interface Networld and its service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate legal mechanisms and safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 12. Third-Party Services and Links

The Services may contain links to or integrations with third-party websites, platforms, models, payment providers, social networks, or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Services, technology, or legal requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date and provide additional notice when required by law.

## 14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Interface Networld through the Contact page on **InterfaceNetworld.com**.
